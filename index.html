Why this design?

        
The scheduler treats budget and bid changes as reusable rules rather than isolated cell edits. Suggestions can be generated from historic performance data, reviewed by the user, then applied directly or edited before becoming active schedule rules.

      

      
    
        
  • The rule list is the source of truth; the grid is a readable preview of the resulting hourly schedule.
    • 
        
  • Applied or adjusted suggestions disappear to avoid duplicate recommendations, while deleted rules can be suggested again.
    • 
        
  • Disregarded suggestions stay hidden so the system does not keep resurfacing rejected ideas.
    • 
        
  • Overlaps are not left ambiguous: users can combine them or let priority order decide which rule applies.
    • 
        
  • Large budget or bid increases require explicit confirmation at the moment of saving the rule.
